Analytics & privacy notice for the website of Shandong Lantian Energy Technology Co., Ltd.

Google Analytics visitor and WhatsApp-click statistics are currently paused. The analytics popup and floating preferences button have been removed. Browsing and WhatsApp contact links continue to work.

Current analytics status ↘

In this notice

01 / Current status

Analytics is paused.

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) measurement is disabled in the current website release. Our analytics module does not load Google's analytics script or send analytics requests, including when a visitor previously accepted analytics.

The optional analytics popup and floating Analytics preferences control are no longer displayed. Their removal does not enable tracking in the background. Browsing the website and choosing a WhatsApp contact link do not require an analytics choice.

A future change to measurement would require a website update and a matching update to this notice; a previously saved acceptance does not override the current pause.

02 / Measurement paused

No new GA4 visits or click events.

The paused module does not send page-view or whatsapp_click events. It does not enable Google advertising storage, Google signals or ad-personalization features, and it does not launch paid advertising.

Pausing website measurement does not erase any information that Google may have received before the pause. It also does not recall requests already in flight on an older open page. Refreshing the website loads the current release.

A click is not a conversation.

A WhatsApp click shows only that a visitor selected a contact link. It does not prove that a message was sent, an enquiry was received or a sale took place. Our analytics cannot read your WhatsApp conversations.

03 / Browser storage

Earlier choices do not restart analytics.

A choice saved by an earlier release may remain in your browser's local storage. It is ignored while analytics is paused and is not used to identify you in reports.

The current module does not create new GA identifier cookies. It attempts to remove the earlier _ga and _ga_P2KJHXDBS2 cookies for this website without removing unrelated cookies. Browser settings may restrict cookie access.

You can remove cookies and local storage using your browser's site-data controls. This does not erase historical data already received by an external service.

04 / Service providers

Understand the external services.

For information about Google's processing of any earlier analytics data, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google's Analytics data and privacy information.

WhatsApp contact links take you to an external WhatsApp service. Your use of that service and any messages you send are separate from our website statistics and are subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy. Choosing a WhatsApp link does not require acceptance of our website analytics.

This notice describes the website analytics implementation. It does not replace the information or terms shown by those external services.
