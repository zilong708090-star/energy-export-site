Analytics & privacy notice for the website of Shandong Lantian Energy Technology Co., Ltd.

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and WhatsApp button clicks. No advertising features are enabled, and you can turn analytics off at any time.

01 / Current status

Analytics is active on this website.

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) measurement ID G-P2KJHXDBS2 is loaded on this website. It records page views and clicks on WhatsApp contact links, so we can understand which products and pages visitors find useful.

The website does not display a cookie consent popup. Analytics runs by default and you can switch it off for this browser using the control in section 03; the switch is also respected on later visits. Browsing and using WhatsApp contact links never depend on analytics.

No advertising features.

Google signals, advertising storage, ad personalisation and remarketing are disabled. Data is not used to build advertising audiences and is not sold. Measurement exists to improve product information and the quotation process.

02 / What is measured

Page views and contact-button clicks.

The measurement sends a page_view event for the page you open and a whatsapp_click event when a WhatsApp contact link is selected, including whether the link sits in the header, the floating button, the contact block or a product page.

These events do not include the WhatsApp message you may write, your phone number, your name or any form content. No account registration is required to browse, and no user identifiers are created for advertising.

Measurement is skipped entirely when your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, or when the analytics script cannot load.

A click is not a conversation.

A WhatsApp click shows only that a visitor selected a contact link. It does not prove that a message was sent, an enquiry was received or a sale took place. Our analytics cannot read your WhatsApp conversations.

03 / Cookies & opt-out

Turn analytics off in this browser.

Analytics may store first-party cookies named _ga and _ga_P2KJHXDBS2, which help distinguish repeat visits for up to 90 days. A single local-storage entry remembers whether you switched analytics off.

Use the control below to switch analytics off in this browser and remove its cookies. The setting is stored locally and applies to this browser only; clearing site data removes the setting and analytics would run again.

Turn analytics off in this browser

You can also block or delete cookies and local storage through your browser's site-data settings. Blocking site data does not prevent you from browsing the website or contacting us.

04 / Service providers

Understand the external services.

For information about Google's processing of any earlier analytics data, see the Google Privacy Policy and Google's Analytics data and privacy information.

WhatsApp contact links take you to an external WhatsApp service. Your use of that service and any messages you send are separate from our website statistics and are subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy. Choosing a WhatsApp link does not require acceptance of our website analytics.

This notice describes the website analytics implementation. It does not replace the information or terms shown by those external services.
