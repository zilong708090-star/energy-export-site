## Composition requirement

Preserve the entire image or a crop proven to keep all foreground blade tips and the complete tower base. `object-fit: contain` is safest. An aggressive square/landscape `cover` crop can recreate the user's complaint by leaving just a rotor and losing the full mast. The tall homepage product card is well suited to this portrait photograph. Use a restrained surrounding background if letterboxing is needed.
