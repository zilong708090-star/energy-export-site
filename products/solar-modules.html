03 Technical review fields

Specify the module.
Validate the evidence.

Use model-level documents for electrical design, structural review and destination-market compliance.

01

Maximum power

Rated output, measurement conditions, tolerance and binning for the selected model.

02

Electrical values

Vmp, Imp, Voc and Isc at the stated test condition and temperature.

03

Temperature behavior

Temperature coefficients and nominal operating temperature data.

04

Cell & construction

Cell technology, layout, glass, encapsulation, back construction and frame.

05

Mechanical format

Dimensions, weight, frame profile, mounting zones and handling requirements.

06

System limits

Maximum voltage, series fuse rating, connector, cable length and polarity.

07

Environmental ratings

Mechanical loading, fire classification and relevant environmental test evidence.

08

Documentation & terms

Certifications, traceability, packing data and applicable warranty documents.

04 Selection sequence

From site constraints
to approved module.

A concise project brief helps identify module formats that fit both the electrical design and the installation method.

01 / DEFINE

Define project conditions

Provide installation type, system size, climate, electrical architecture, mounting system and destination market.

02 / COMPARE

Compare compatible formats

Review power, dimensions, current, monofacial or bifacial construction, logistics and installation constraints.

03 / APPROVE

Approve the selected model

Verify datasheet, drawings, certifications, connector details, packing information, warranty documents and quotation.
