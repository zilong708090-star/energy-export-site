03 Technical review fields

Every interface
has to agree.

Confirm each value against the selected-model datasheet, compatibility list and destination-market requirements.

01

Rated AC output

Continuous and peak power, current, phase, voltage, frequency and power factor.

02

PV input window

Maximum DC voltage, startup voltage, MPPT range and permitted array oversizing.

03

MPPT allocation

Tracker quantity, strings per tracker and maximum current or short-circuit current.

04

Battery interface

Supported voltage range, charge/discharge limits and approved communications.

05

Backup operation

Function availability, transfer behavior, supported load type and output limits.

06

Protection & metering

Required AC/DC protection, external meter, zero-export control and isolation.

07

Monitoring & control

LAN, wireless or fieldbus interfaces, portal functions and third-party integration.

08

Environment & compliance

Enclosure rating, derating, noise, grid approvals and market documentation.

04 Selection sequence

From string plan
to approved model.

A useful review brings the PV array, grid connection, load and battery requirements into one conversation.

01 / MAP

Map the electrical design

Provide PV module data, string plan, grid voltage and phase, load profile, battery requirements and export objective.

02 / SELECT

Select the architecture

Choose grid-tied or hybrid operation and review MPPT allocation, backup, monitoring, protection and interfaces.

03 / VERIFY

Verify model-level details

Confirm final ratings, supported functions, compatibility, compliance documents and commercial scope.
