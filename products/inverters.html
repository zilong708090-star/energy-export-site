03 The complete conversion system

Good power conversion
starts with good integration.

The inverter works at the center of the energy system. Its product format, PV inputs, battery communication and protection need to suit the same installation.

Equipment layout

A format that fits the project

Choose wall-mounted or cabinet equipment around installation space, ventilation, cable access and maintenance requirements.

PV input design

Let the array guide the inputs

Account for roof orientation, shading, string lengths, module current and voltage when arranging the inverter inputs.

Storage & backup

Make the battery connection explicit

Review supported battery communication, charge and discharge settings, reserve energy and the circuits assigned to backup.

Technical handover

Prepare for local commissioning

Bring together the wiring information, protection layout, meter placement, monitoring access and startup checks for the installer.

04 Engineering & procurement

Every interface
has to agree.

Bring the array, battery, grid and load requirements into one equipment schedule, using the selected inverter’s datasheet and compatibility information.

01

Rated AC output

Continuous and peak power, current, phase, voltage, frequency and power factor.

02

PV input window

Maximum DC voltage, startup voltage, MPPT range and permitted array oversizing.

03

MPPT allocation

Tracker quantity, strings per tracker and maximum current or short-circuit current.

04

Battery interface

Supported voltage range, charge/discharge limits and approved communications.

05

Backup operation

Function availability, transfer behavior, supported load type and output limits.

06

Protection & metering

Required AC/DC protection, external meter, zero-export control and isolation.

07

Monitoring & control

LAN, wireless or fieldbus interfaces, portal functions and third-party integration.

08

Environment & compliance

Enclosure rating, derating, noise, grid approvals and market documentation.
