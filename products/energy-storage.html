01 Design around the operating objective

Treat storage as a
complete site system.

Power, energy, controls, thermal management, protection and site integration have to work together. The right starting point is the load profile—not a catalogue number.

01 / SIZE

Match power and energy independently

Configure around peak demand, renewable-energy shifting, backup duration or scheduled charge and discharge requirements.

02 / INTEGRATE

Review every system interface

Evaluate the battery, PCS, EMS, switchgear, communications and site electrical design as one coordinated architecture.

03 / DEPLOY

Plan for real site conditions

Temperature, utility rules, fire-safety requirements, operating strategy and maintenance access shape the final configuration.

02 Application pathways

One platform.
Different jobs.

Define the value stream and operating priority before model selection. Multiple objectives can be combined when the site architecture supports them.

A / 01

Peak-demand management

A / 02

Solar self-consumption

Move surplus PV production into later operating hours.

A / 03

Critical-load backup

Plan around defined loads, autonomy targets and transfer architecture.

A / 04

Time-of-use management

Schedule charging and discharge around site demand and tariff windows.

A / 05

C&I microgrids

Coordinate storage with PV, wind, generators, controls and site loads.

A / 06

Managed EV charging

Support charging demand where site connection capacity is constrained.

03 Technical review fields

Confirm the model.
Then confirm the claim.

Each field below belongs in a selected-model datasheet or project review. It is intentionally not presented as a universal product promise.

01

Rated power

Continuous and peak AC power, charge and discharge limits, and operating modes.

02

Nominal & usable energy

Energy definition, state-of-charge window, depth of discharge and reserve strategy.

03

Battery architecture

Cell chemistry, module design, nominal voltage and permitted operating range.

04

Coupling & interfaces

AC- or DC-coupled arrangement, PCS compatibility and balance-of-system scope.

05

Thermal management

Cooling or heating method, control strategy, auxiliary use and environmental limits.

06

Controls & communications

EMS functions, protocols, monitoring, third-party interfaces and remote access.

07

Protection & safety

Electrical protection, isolation, fire strategy, emergency response and site controls.

08

Market documentation

Grid code, transport documents, certification scope and destination-market evidence.

04 Selection sequence

From site data
to selected model.

A useful quotation starts with enough project context to identify technical mismatches early.

01 / ASSESS

Assess the site

Share the load profile, grid connection, renewable system, installation environment, available space and operating objectives.

02 / DEFINE

Define the configuration

Align power, usable energy, coupling architecture, operating strategy, controls and balance-of-system requirements.

03 / VERIFY

Confirm the selected model

Review the datasheet, interfaces, protection scheme, compliance documents, commercial terms and delivery scope.
