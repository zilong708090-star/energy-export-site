01 Design around the operating objective

Treat storage as a
complete site system.

Power, energy, controls, thermal management, protection and site integration have to work together. The right starting point is the load profile—not a catalogue number.

01 / SIZE

Match power and energy independently

Configure around peak demand, renewable-energy shifting, backup duration or scheduled charge and discharge requirements.

02 / INTEGRATE

Review every system interface

Evaluate the battery, PCS, EMS, switchgear, communications and site electrical design as one coordinated architecture.

03 / DEPLOY

Plan for real site conditions

Temperature, utility rules, fire-safety requirements, operating strategy and maintenance access shape the final configuration.

04 Engineering & procurement

A clear specification.
A coordinated system.

Use the project brief and equipment documentation to align storage capacity, interfaces and installation responsibilities before procurement.

01

Rated power

Continuous and peak AC power, charge and discharge limits, and operating modes.

02

Nominal & usable energy

Energy definition, state-of-charge window, depth of discharge and reserve strategy.

03

Battery architecture

Cell chemistry, module design, nominal voltage and permitted operating range.

04

Coupling & interfaces

AC- or DC-coupled arrangement, PCS compatibility and balance-of-system scope.

05

Thermal management

Cooling or heating method, control strategy, auxiliary use and environmental limits.

06

Controls & communications

EMS functions, protocols, monitoring, third-party interfaces and remote access.

07

Protection & safety

Electrical protection, isolation, fire strategy, emergency response and site controls.

08

Market documentation

Grid code, transport documents, certification scope and destination-market evidence.
